# Chapter divider

Reusable full-frame separators between episode chapters.

## Variants

- `delegation` — light editorial composition. Domain labels delegate from the root and resolve into the chapter title.
- `route` — dark technical composition. A packet crosses resolver nodes and unlocks the next chapter.

## Copy rules

- Keep the title to one or two semantic lines.
- Prefer 1–4 words per line and no more than 24 characters on the longest line.
- `eyebrow` names the topic; `detail` carries protocol vocabulary or a short route.
- The chapter title is the only primary focal point.

Both references are authored for 120 frames at 30 fps and a 2560×1440 output composition.
